10 Trading Principles

Learn discipline, risk management and patience.

01. Control Your Emotions

Knowledge helps you find opportunities. Discipline and emotional control help you survive in the market.

02. Think Long Term

Build capital, invest smart and give time in the market a chance to work.

03. Trade With a Plan

Trading without a plan exposes your capital to avoidable decisions. Define your setup before entering.

04. Protect Your Capital

One bad trading day can undo years of hard work. Focus on survival, learning and consistency.

05. Master Boredom

Avoid chasing excitement. Patience and consistency are important parts of disciplined trading.

06. Risk Small

Keep risk controlled on every trade. Position sizing and risk limits help you stay in the game.

07. Manage Your Emotions

When emotions rise, decision-making can suffer. Follow a written plan rather than reacting impulsively.

08. Treat Trading Like a Business

Plan, execute, review and improve. Approach trading with responsibility and discipline.

09. Protect Your Trading Account

There will always be another opportunity, but lost capital can be difficult to recover.

10. Play the Long-Term Game

Stock market wealth-building is a long journey. Learn patiently and focus on sustainable habits.
